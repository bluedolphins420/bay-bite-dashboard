# Bay Area Fishing Kit — Exact Product Winners, Version 3.1

## Panel verdict

Two spinning systems remain the correct minimum. The first draft was **not safe to buy unchanged** because its heavy rod was rated below its listed sinker-plus-bait payload. Two independent reviewers audited every purchase, method, species claim, price guardrail, landing tool, safety item, and regulation statement.

This revision chooses the **best complete two-pole architecture and exact products at a reasonable price**, not whatever search result or multipack happens to be cheapest. Product decisions use manufacturer specifications, observed August 2026 prices, independent testing where available, failure cost, local mechanical fit, and parts/support.

1. A 7-foot medium-light lake/finesse combo.
2. A true 10-foot, 1–5 oz surf rod with a salt-capable 5000 reel.

A cheaper 9-foot PENN Wrath II 6000 combo remains a valid *light-surf-only* alternative, but its 1–3 oz rod cannot safely cast 4 oz plus bait and therefore is not the canonical recommendation.

## Price snapshot and limits

Observed prices are time-sensitive and do not prove Prime eligibility or delivery to Daly City.

- **Exact-product universal core:** modeled target range **$605–$700**. The low end uses the Fierce sale and local lead; $700 is the all-in procurement stop, not a suggestion to spend more.
- **All venue modules together:** modeled **$141–$175**, but buy only the module required before a specific trip.
- **Full all-venue system:** modeled target **$746–$875**.
- Buying every item at every individual hard ceiling would reach roughly **$784 core / $959 full**. Do not do that: wait for sales, use the listed fallback, or defer duplicate lure colors before downgrading line, hooks, landing gear, PFD, or casting protection.
- Treat the upper figures as hard-category ceilings, not spending targets. Exact-product prices below are **buy-at-or-below thresholds**, not promises.
- Do not pay extra merely to force every sinker or mono spool onto Amazon; exact weights are often cheaper at a local tackle shop.
- Amazon was checked signed out. Verify seller, return policy, Prime delivery, exact variant, and checkout price in your own account before ordering.

# 1. The two poles

## Pole 1 — lakes, finesse, and light Bay work

- [ ] [PENN Wrath II spinning combo — exact 7-foot/3000 Amazon product](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0C8K1DS6H)

**Required variant:** model `WRTHII3000702ML`; 7 ft; two-piece; medium-light; extra-fast; 3000 reel; 6–12 lb line; 1/16–5/8 oz lure rating. PENN's official table confirms this configuration.[4]

**Buy at:** $70–$80. **Do not exceed:** $80. Confirm model `WRTHII3000702ML`, not the 6'6" 2500 variant shown first on some Amazon pages. The panel observed exact ASIN `B0C8K1DS6H` at $69.99, but still verify the model before checkout.[4][8]

**Why this wins:** two-piece portability, salt-capable graphite reel body, insert-free Dura-Guides, useful 1/16–5/8 oz range, and replaceable PENN parts. The reel's 2+1 bearings and felt drag are basic, but acceptable on the light system. A Shimano Sienna combo may feel smoother, but no exact two-piece 7-foot medium-light salt-capable Amazon variant was verified at a better delivered price; do not substitute based on title alone.

**Spool with:** 15 lb braid over inexpensive mono backing.

**Jobs:** stocked trout, crappie, bluegill, finesse bass, ordinary carp and catfish, floats, small jigs, inline spinners, light spoons, surfperch Carolina rigs, jacksmelt, and light pier work.

**Important limitation:** total jighead plus soft-plastic weight must remain at or below 5/8 oz. Use Pole 2 for heavier Bay lures, sinkers, current, or structure.

## Pole 2 — open surf, heavier bait, and stronger fish

- [ ] [PENN Squadron IV 10-foot surf rod — exact-model Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=SQDSFIV1530S10)
- [ ] [PENN Fierce IV 5000 spinning reel — current sale search](https://www.dickssportinggoods.com/search/SearchDisplay?searchTerm=PENN%20Fierce%20IV%205000)

**Rod requirement:** model `SQDSFIV1530S10`; 10 ft; two-piece; medium-heavy; 15–30 lb; **1–5 oz total casting payload**. Amazon displayed approximately $109.98 during review.[5]

**Reel winner:** `FRCIV5000`; full-metal body and sideplate; four sealed stainless bearings plus instant anti-reverse; HT-100 carbon drag; Superline spool. An August 5 sale listed it at $67.49—exceptional value below the lighter graphite Pursuit.[9]

**Targets:** rod $95–$110, Fierce reel $67–$90. **Combined hard ceiling:** $200. If the Fierce rises above $100, buy the exact [PENN Pursuit V 5000, `PURV5000`, ASIN `B0DNTVCF77`](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DNTVCF77) at $65–$80 instead; its graphite body is lighter and corrosion-resistant but flexes more under heavy load.[6]

**Spool with:** 30 lb braid over mono backing.

**Jobs:** Pacifica/Ocean Beach bait fishing, surfperch high-low rigs, striped bass, halibut, croaker, flounder/sole, heavier catfish, and controlled encounters with modest sharks or rays.

**Casting rule:** the rod's 5 oz ceiling includes **sinker + bait + rig**. A 4 oz sinker permits only a small bait/rig payload; do not exceed the printed rod rating.

**Fallback rod:** [Daiwa Emcast 10-foot MH 1–6 oz search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Daiwa+Emcast+EMCST1002MHFS+10+foot+1-6+oz) only if the exact `EMCST1002MHFS` variant costs less than the Squadron.[7]

# 8. Rig recipes

## Trout dough-bait rig — Pole 1

15 lb braid → swivel → 8 lb leader, 18–36 inches → #8 baitholder. Place a 1/4–1/2 oz egg sinker on the braid above the swivel. Use just enough floating dough to cover the hook.

## Trout/panfish float rig — Pole 1

15 lb braid → bobber stop → bead → slip float → small split shot → 8 lb leader → #8 hook or 1/16 oz jig.

## Bass Texas rig — Pole 1

15 lb braid → 1/8 or 1/4 oz bullet weight → 3/0 EWG → 5-inch green-pumpkin stick worm.

## Bass drop-shot — Pole 1

15 lb braid → 8 lb leader → size 1/1/0 drop-shot hook → 12–18 inches to 1/8 or 1/4 oz drop-shot weight.

## Surfperch Carolina rig — Pole 1 or 2

Braid → 1/2–1.5 oz egg sinker → bead → swivel → 18–24 inches of 8 lb mono → #8 or #2 baitholder → Gulp sandworm or local bait.

## Surf high-low rig — Pole 2

30 lb braid → 50 lb shock leader → 30 lb mono rig body → two droppers with #2 hooks → 2–4 oz pyramid sinker. Keep total payload at or below 5 oz.

## Striper/halibut fish-finder rig — Pole 2

30 lb braid → 50 lb shock leader → sinker slide and 2–4 oz pyramid → bead → 60 lb swivel → 18–30 inches of 30 lb mono → 1/0 or 3/0 inline circle hook.

## Modest shark/ray rig — Pole 2, optional module

30 lb braid → 50 lb shock leader → sinker slide → swivel → 3–6 ft of 50 lb mono → 5/0 or 6/0 inline circle hook. Use proper landing gear and a partner; cut the leader when safe control is not possible.

# 10. Deliberate exclusions

Do not add these to the universal kit:

- Dedicated sturgeon gear.
- Salmon-specific tackle.
- Dungeness-crab snare/hoop gear.
- Giant-shark or giant-ray equipment.
- Poke-poling equipment.
- Dedicated lingcod/rockfish tackle.
- Baitcaster, carp hair-rig system, trout ultralight, or trophy-catfish rod.
- Giant lure assortments, hook assortments, premium fluorocarbon, or a third general-purpose pole.

Those are separate fisheries, not missing pieces of this system.

# 11. Current regulation and maintenance notes

In the San Francisco Bay District, surfperch is open August 1, 2026 through March 31, 2027; the adjacent Central Management Area open coast is open year-round.[1][2] White sturgeon fishing in the Bay District is closed July 1–September 30, 2026; outside that closure, current white-sturgeon rules remain catch-and-release only. Green sturgeon may never be taken or removed from the water.[1]

Inside the Bay, the current general rule is one line with no more than three hooks or lures, except qualifying public piers permit no more than two rods and lines. Qualifying public piers retain a license exemption, but all other rules still apply.[1][3]

Before every trip, open the live CDFW page for the exact water. Do not rely on this shopping document as a permanent regulation source.

After saltwater use: snug the drag, apply only a gentle freshwater mist, wipe and air-dry, then loosen the drag for storage. Never dunk the reel or blast it with pressure. Periodically clean and lightly lubricate the line roller, handle joints, and bail pivots according to the reel manual.
